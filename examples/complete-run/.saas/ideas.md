## Survivor 1: FX Invoice Ledger
Difference sentence: Shows fee-adjusted earnings at invoice time, not after month-end accounting.
Scores: novelty 3.8, usefulness 4.6, evidence 4.2, surprise 3.5.
Biggest risk: freelancers may tolerate spreadsheets.
Cheapest first test: fake-door pricing on a landing page with invoice calculator.
Trend signals: Product Hunt invoicing category shows active launch demand (2026-10-30); freelancer fee complaints recur in public forums (2026-09-14, UNVERIFIED exact URL).
Launch scope: single-page invoice form, currency selector, fee input, net earnings summary, saved invoice list. No bank sync, no tax engine, no team features.

## Survivor 2: Translator Quote Guard
Difference sentence: Prices translation jobs by word count, language pair, and currency volatility before the quote is sent.
Scores: novelty 3.6, usefulness 4.0, evidence 3.7, surprise 3.4.
Biggest risk: crowded quoting-tool market.
Cheapest first test: collect 20 translator quotes with current pricing method.
Trend signals: Product Hunt and Indie Hackers show ongoing micro-tool launches for freelancers (2026-08-20, UNVERIFIED exact URL); demand is present but less specific than invoice fee pain.
Launch scope: quote calculator, saved rate card, exportable quote PDF. No CRM, no payment collection, no client portal.

## Rejected
- Generic freelancer accounting app: no gap against Xero/QuickBooks; slow to launch well.
- AI translation agency: out of scope, heavy build, marketplace/cold-start risk, weak wedge.
- Crypto invoice platform: regulatory and trust burden too high for a quick MVP.
