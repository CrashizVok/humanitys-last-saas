# Headlines

## 1. See what you actually earned
- Subhead: Track invoices in one currency and payouts in another, with fees included.
- Use when: targeting translators who already invoice internationally.

## 2. Invoice in EUR, know your USD
- Subhead: Record the payout, subtract the fee, and keep the real margin per job.
- Use when: ads or landing pages focused on currency mismatch.

## 3. A small ledger for cross-currency freelance work
- Subhead: No accounting suite. Just invoices, payouts, and net earnings.
- Use when: users are overwhelmed by full accounting tools.
