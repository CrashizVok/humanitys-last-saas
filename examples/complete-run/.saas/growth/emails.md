# Onboarding emails

## Email 1 - Welcome
- Send timing: immediately after signup
- Subject: Your first invoice, in two currencies
- Preview: See the fee-adjusted amount before you send it.
- Body: Create your first invoice, enter the payout you expect, and FX Invoice Ledger shows the net amount after fees.
- CTA: Create an invoice

## Email 2 - Core action nudge
- Send timing: day 2 if no invoice created
- Subject: Check one real payout
- Preview: A minute is enough to see the margin.
- Body: Pick one recent job. Enter the invoice amount, the payout amount, and the fee. You will see what you actually earned.
- CTA: Record a payout

## Email 3 - Feedback / fake-door pricing interest
- Send timing: day 5
- Subject: Would you pay for this?
- Preview: Tell us what the paid version should include.
- Body: We are testing paid plans. If you click through, you can register interest. The button records intent and shows "Coming soon" without charging.
- CTA: See pricing
