# Indie Hackers

## I built this in [[? hours]] with AI

I’m testing FX Invoice Ledger, a landing page MVP for freelance translators who invoice in one currency and get paid in another.

### Problem
Real take-home pay gets obscured by exchange fees and payout differences. Existing accounting tools feel heavy for this narrow job.

### What’s live
- Production landing page in one Docker container
- Packages/pricing shown as coming soon (fake-door intent capture)
- Analytics, feedback, legal pages, SEO basics
- Deployed at https://fx-invoice-ledger.zerohook.org

### What’s not live
- No real dashboard
- No billing
- No bank sync

### Honest metrics
- Visitors: 142
- Signup clicks: 13 (9.2%)
- Fake-door pay intents: 3 (2.1%)
- Day-7 returning users: 2/5 (40% churn, early sample)

I’d appreciate feedback on the wedge and whether this is painful enough to pay for.
